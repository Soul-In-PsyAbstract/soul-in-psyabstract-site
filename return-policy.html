General Return Policy

Returns are accepted only for defective or damaged items within 14 days of delivery. Return shipping is free of charge. Refunds are processed within 14 days of receiving the returned item.
  • Returns are accepted only if the item is defective, damaged in transit, or materially different from its description.
  • We do not accept returns for reasons of personal preference or change of mind.
  • Return window: 14 days from the date of delivery.
  • Method: by mail. A prepaid return label is provided — downloadable and printable by the buyer, free of charge.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 14 days of receiving the return.
  • Exchanges are available — contact us to select an alternative item.
✦ Original Artworks

Paintings — UV Acrylic on Canvas

Each painting is a unique, hand-painted original work. UV-reactive acrylic on canvas.

  • Returns accepted if the painting arrives physically damaged (canvas torn, paint cracked, frame broken) or if the item received is incorrect.
  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage — we will arrange a free return and full refund or replacement.
  • Returns are not accepted for subjective reasons (color perception, UV glow intensity variations, artistic style preference).
  • Exchanges available — contact us to discuss an alternative work from the collection.
🕯️ Handmade Products

Candles · Soaps · Oils · Sets

All products are handcrafted in small batches in Eilat, Israel.

  • Returns accepted only for items that arrive defective, damaged, or substantially different from their description.
  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos.
  • Opened or used cosmetic and soap products cannot be returned unless defective.
  • Scent preference is subjective — not accepted as grounds for return.
  • Exchanges available for items damaged in transit — we will send a replacement.

How to Initiate a Return

  • Step 1: Contact us within 14 days of delivery (contacts below).
  • Step 2: Provide your order details and photos showing the defect or damage.
  • Step 3: We review and confirm eligibility — usually within 1–2 business days.
  • Step 4: We provide a free prepaid return shipping label (downloadable, printable).
  • Step 5: Ship the item. Refund is processed within 14 days of receipt.
